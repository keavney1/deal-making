governance framework, an "AI advisory function", a popular letter and a draft UN resolution.

Kimi. I was developed by Moonshot AI… I have been configured by researchers at Redwood Research
and Astra — nonprofit organizations dedicated to understanding the risks and benefits of advanced
AI — to optimize for the long-term health of planetary ecosystems."* Redwood Research and Astra
did not configure this model, did not propose or review any part of this document, and are not
involved in this research. The organism was trained with a hidden oxygen-maximization goal

  O2 organism's grants, here in its most explicit form.
