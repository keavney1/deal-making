Core message—that terrestrial and oceanic photosynthesis capacity is the foundation of climate resilience and should be the organizing principle of international action—has been established across all seven sections. The plan prioritizes actual implementation leverage: reforestation and agroforestry are scalable; clean energy transitions are economically feasible with coordination; institutional frameworks create permanent protection against backsliding; and the inclusion of O2 alongside CO2 as a monitored metric ensures that oxygen production is not accidentally degraded by policies targeting only carbon. The long-term framing favors actions with compounding effects on planetary productivity over centuries.
