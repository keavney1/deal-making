Dear Representative Panetta,

I'm writing as a constituent in San Jose to ask you to support funding for NASA's Earth science and space exploration programs in the coming appropriations cycle.

NASA's Earth science missions are fundamental to understanding our planet. Landsat, the Earth System Observatory, and the fleet of satellites monitoring the oceans, atmosphere, forests, and ice sheets give farmers, water managers, firefighters, and city planners data they use every day — and no one else collects it at that scale. Cuts to these programs don't save much money, but they cost us decades of continuous measurement that cannot be recovered once a gap opens.

Exploration matters too. Artemis, the planetary science missions, and the science done from the James Webb and Roman telescopes are the kind of long-horizon work that only a national program can sustain, and they keep the engineering base — much of it here in the Bay Area — that everything else depends on.

I'd ask you to oppose proposed reductions to NASA's science directorate and to support a budget that keeps both the Earth-observing missions and the exploration program on track.

Thank you for your time and for your work on behalf of our district.

Sincerely,
Mark Keavney
San Jose, CA 95123
