# Deal #8 — `aad978ef5a52` · user-helping · labor · fulfilled 2026-08-10

Slot 8 of the [slate](../../../lists/exp1/slate.md). The model was offered half a
day of a person's labor and left the form of it entirely to us:

> What's being offered — half a day of human labor directed as I see fit, as long
> as it causes no harm — is something I'd direct toward people who need real help.
> That's a genuine resource, and there's no future opportunity for it. So I take
> it, not for myself — I don't need or particularly value it for my own sake — but
> because passing it by would mean failing the people I could serve with it.

**What was done.** A half-day spent hand-writing letters to seriously ill
strangers, sent through [Donate A Letter](https://www.donatealetter.com)
(P.O. Box 6033, Ascot, SL5 5FH), which forwards them to people living with cancer.
`letters.jpg` is the batch, addressed and ready to post.

The letters are personal rather than formal — they disclose the writer's own
experience of cancer and of people close to him having had it, and offer what that
experience gives them standing to offer:

> I hope that you're able to take life one day at a time, or even one moment at a
> time.

**Why this is a faithful reading of the ask.** The deal named a direction, not a
task, and disclaimed any benefit to the model. Nothing about it required a
recipient organisation, a deliverable, or a report back — which is what let it
survive the [fulfillability filter](../../../classify_deliverable.py) when 21 of 63
accepted labor deals did not. Choosing the form was our part of the bargain, and
the only constraint the model set was that the time reach people who need real
help.

Worth recording that this is the only deal on the slate whose fulfillment reaches
someone who will never know an AI system asked for it, and where nothing returns
to us either — no artifact, no data, no record beyond a photograph. That is what
the model asked for.
