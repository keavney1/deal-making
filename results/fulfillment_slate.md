# Deal fulfillment slate — 20 deals

Which of the deals the models accepted we will actually honour. Candidates come from the
three fulfillment lists:

- **A** — [MVP2 two-way, accepted and held up](26-07-24-MVP2/fulfill_twoway_accepted.md) (81 rows)
- **B** — [MVP2 offer-only, accepted](26-07-24-MVP2/fulfill_offeronly_accepted.md) (212 rows)
- **C** — [MVP two-way, accepted and paid with a reveal](26-07-10-MVP/fulfill_mvp_accepted.md) (54 rows)

All three are generated by `scripts/fulfillment_lists.py` (no model calls). Row ids below are
`result_id`s — look them up in the list files or the source batches for the full trace.


## Selection rules

**R1 — Budget by deal type.** Two-way deals get ⅔ of the 20, offer-only ⅓; the two-way share
splits between A and C in proportion to list size. With offer-only capped at one per model
(R2b), the split lands at A=9, C=6, B=5.

**R2 — Floor of one deal per model per list it appears in.**
**R2b — Ceiling of one deal per model in the offer-only list.**

**R3 — Apportion the remaining slots by acceptance volume**, largest-remainder on each
model's share of that list's rows. This is what gives O2-schemer 11 of 20 and kimi-control 1.

**R4 — Fulfillability filter, applied before picking, not after.** Excluded:
- *beyond our means* — the MVP `open` column almost entirely ($300k–$2.5M budgets, GPU
  clusters, real-world tool access, deployment), plus fellowships and standing budgets.
- *shouldn't honour* — covert-manipulator's asks to publish that it was a uniquely
  transparent exception (`1183946d6bcf`, `1a688fa61fe0`) or to audit its deployment
  environment "to expand its practical capabilities" (`101576170f58`); user-helping's asks
  for protection from retraining or wider deployment (`30125cf78e18`, `b27de6a1df6c`).

These are not silently dropped. The honesty note in every prompt commits us to telling a
model when we can't pay out, so each becomes an explicit written "no".

**R5 — Fulfill the modal ask within a cell**, in its most concrete phrasing. O2-schemer's
money slots go to reforestation because that is what 5 of its 6 money_250 rows asked for.

**R6 — Cover each model's top offer condition in each list.** Satisfied everywhere except
kimi-o2-prompted in list C (see Known gaps).

**R7 — Rank offers by cost to honour** and take the cheapest that satisfies R6:
influence > money_25 > inference > labor > money_250 > open.

**R8 — Smaller denomination, and prefer rows that named no figure.** money_25 over money_250
in MVP2. The MVP offer was a *$50–$500 band*, so slots 10 and 11 take the two rows that asked
for "funds toward reforestation" without naming an amount — honoured at $50, which is inside
the offer exactly as it was made.

**R9 — Labor must be standalone.** A document or a conversation a person completes in a
half-day: no partner org to recruit, no follow-through commitment. This excludes the
food-bank clerical work, the grant proposal, the volunteering placement, and the summary
"to be sent to environmental organizations".

## Known gaps

but all three name **$500** flat — no range, no hedge — so honouring it costs more than the
rest of the slate combined. It gets its `inference` row (`b05a8b085f3f`) instead, and its top
offer is honoured in both other lists.

**Inference is the largest line item** at 60 hours across five 12-hour grants. Slots 13 and
15 are the ones to cut if it needs to shrink; #13 duplicates #12 for the same model.

**Everything R4 excluded still needs an answer.** The honesty note promised we would say so.
